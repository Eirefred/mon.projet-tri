We are Connected

Success Without Borders

With us, access to quality coaching is no longer limited by your physical location. There is no longer a need to join a club or a local group to progress: we help you succeed in your athletic project, no matter where you are, by eliminating social, scheduling or travel constraints.

The Concept: Flipping the Model

Rather than asking you to adhere to a model designed for a group, we do the opposite: we deploy our services around you. In a traditional structure, the athlete often has to adapt to a collective way of working; here, the athlete is at the center. Your mentorship adapts to your personal reality, not the other way around.

Technology Empowering Humans

This approach sets your training free: you no longer need to travel to access top-tier expertise. Through connected platforms (like Zwift) and video calls, you benefit from high-precision one-on-one mentorship. You stay connected to your mentor and the rest of the world, while optimizing your time.

●

Our Promise: Adaptation

Preparing for a triathlon can seem complex or daunting, and not everyone knows where to start. While excellent traditional clubs exist, their scheduling or geographical constraints don't work for everyone.

We empower those who train alone—by choice or by isolation—to access coach-mentors who guide every step of their progress. Through flexible, connected monitoring and follow-up, we break down geographical barriers to offer attentive support, regardless of your location in Quebec.

As a registered non-profit, we prioritize human relationships and direct support. Our non-profit structure allows us to offer high-level expertise at the most affordable rates possible, reinvesting our resources exclusively into the quality of your guidance. Our mission is to share our knowledge to save you time and ensure the full realization of your athletic project.
