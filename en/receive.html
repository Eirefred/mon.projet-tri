Our Trusted Partners

We rigorously select exceptional partners who enhance your athletic experience: major events recognized for their organizational quality, dedicated healthcare professionals, and equipment providers offering exclusive benefits to members of our network.

📅 Selected Events

Discover our list of partner competitions, renowned for their high-quality courses, safety, and unique atmosphere.

Triathlon de la Vallée-du-Richelieu One of the very first triathlons of the season! Held mainly at the beautiful Domaine International de Rouville, this human-scale triathlon is welcoming and accessible. Open-water swimming takes place in a man-made basin featuring a large central fountain. On the bike leg, participants ride along peaceful, low-traffic countryside roads. All of this comes with a friendly, welcoming, and intimate atmosphere.
View details ↗
Triathlon Duathlon de Saint-Lambert A top favorite among beginners, largely thanks to its pool swim format. Organized early in the season, it offers a great opportunity to get into the groove before taking on bigger summer challenges. Its multi-lap course in the heart of Saint-Lambert allows athletes to enjoy continuous cheers and support from enthusiastic spectators.
View details ↗
Triathlon Duathlon de Verdun Held in mid-summer, this triathlon brings a true summer holiday vibe at Parc du Quai-de-La Tortue in Verdun, along the St. Lawrence River. The swim portion takes place directly in the river, providing a unique and exhilarating experience due to the current. Organized by a highly experienced crew, it delivers a unique and seamlessly managed event!
View details ↗
Triathlon-Duathlon de Boucherville As the final event of the season, the Boucherville Triathlon-Duathlon was awarded the Triathlon Québec "Provincial Event of the Year" in 2023 and 2025. It offers a friendly, safe environment for athletes competing in their first race. The pool swim ensures a reassuring setting, complemented by the best post-race meal 🍽️ in the province catered by Chez Lionel.
View details ↗
