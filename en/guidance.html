Coaching & Mentorship

A human, accessible, and personalized guidance to reach your goals with confidence.

Tailored & Connected Guidance 🤝

We offer a unique sports coaching service that combines human mentorship with rigorous coaching. To guarantee the highest quality support, we exclusively collaborate with coaches who are NCCP certified (National Coaching Certification Program) or officially recognized by Triathlon Québec.

Our strength lies in customization: we conduct a rigorous coach selection process based on their background and core philosophy. We then carefully pair the right coach with the right athlete to ensure your goals, schedule, and personality match perfectly with your coach.

🏆 Guidance Programs

Relevant criteria and affordable rates.

As a nonprofit organization, our mission is to make triathlon accessible to all. That's why we keep prices as low as possible. We have carefully established coaching criteria focused on what is truly essential, especially when guiding and reassuring beginners.

Beginner

First Step

"One day, I'll do one..."

For those who have never dared to stand at a starting line due to fear or lack of guidance. Enjoy a reassuring structure from start to finish with mentors who have been in your shoes. This program is at the core of our mission: getting you across your first finish line!

View Pricing & Details ▼
  • Dedicated mentor assigned based on your goals.
  • Full assistance when buying your first equipment.
  • 12-week training plan adapted to your current fitness level.
  • Flexible plan adjustments to fit your lifestyle.
  • Detailed breakdown of race proceedings and rules.
  • Comprehensive moral and psychological support for race day.

Training plan + 3 mentorship sessions (40 min each).

FULLY SUPPORTED :

Intermediate & Advanced

Performance

"I want to structure my growth and perform."

Whether you're aiming to optimize your fitness for the local season or take on the ultimate challenge of an Ironman, benefit from expert, highly connected mentorship tailored precisely to your seasonal ambitions.

View Pricing & Details ▼
  • Dedicated mentor matched to your goals (Sprint to Ironman).
  • Personalized and evolving training plan (built in 4-week blocks).
  • Up to 2 adjustments per month based on your availability and fitness level.
  • Regular individual video calls to track progress. ↳ Communication every 2 days during the first two weeks, followed by video calls every 2 to 4 weeks, depending on your needs.
  • In-depth data analysis (TrainingPeaks*) to optimize performance.
  • Targeted race nutrition and pacing strategy.

Ideal to progress with structure, discipline, and motivation.

FULL YEAR :

Advanced data analysis and strategy (70.3 & Ironman).
