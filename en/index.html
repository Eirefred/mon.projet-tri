For Athletes

Our mission is to grow triathlon in Quebec. Whether you are a independant athlete, looking for coaching, or wanting to join a club, we are here to guide you, free of charge.

Our types of resources at your disposal

Find the type of resource tailored to your needs.

Resource A
Self-Guided
"I want to get into triathlon on my own"

Access our complete toolbox to start at your own pace: triathlon introduction, official regulations, season schedules, as well as a selection of safe locations to train in open water, cycling, or running in Quebec, and much more! You can even share your own tips!

Guidance
"I want personalized guidance"

We can guide you every step of the way or simply assist you when needed. To do so, we match you with a coach who will primarily act as a dedicated guide to support your triathlon journey. We select the right profile and ensure capped rates.

Club
"I want to train with a group"

Joining a group means becoming part of a vibrant community to train, progress, and share a rewarding experience. We help you find a club that best matches your philosophy, goals, and location.

For Partners

Do you contribute to the vitality of triathlon in Quebec while promoting your organization? Let's team up.

Coaches & Clubs
"I run a club" "I coach athletes"

Let's connect and define your coaching profile or club philosophy. We will connect you with athletes who fit your vision.

Events
"I organize a race or triathlon"

Energize your events: we manage the welcome process for beginner athletes, and you benefit from exclusive activities hosted by mon Projet Tri. (my Tri Project).

Health & Care
"I am a therapist, physio, or nutritionist"

Become the go-to practitioner for triathletes to assist them with their preparation, recovery, and therapeutic care.

Equipment Providers
"I sell gear or sports products"

Do you sell equipment or products for triathletes? Help make triathlon more accessible while gaining exposure. Let's talk.
